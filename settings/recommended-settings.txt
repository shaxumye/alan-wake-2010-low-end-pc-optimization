ALAN WAKE (2010) - RECOMMENDED SETTINGS
========================================

TARGET SYSTEM
-------------
Dell Latitude 7290
Intel Core i5-8350U
16 GB RAM
Intel integrated graphics (commonly UHD Graphics 620)

START WITH
----------
Resolution:       1280 x 720
Display mode:     Windowed (launcher passes -window)
Anti-aliasing:    Off
Shadows:          Low
Effects:          Low
Post-processing:  Low
Textures:         Low or Medium (test both)
V-Sync:           Off for testing

IF IT STILL STUTTERS
--------------------
Try the included 960 x 540 launcher.
Lower shadows/effects before lowering textures further.
Close browsers and GPU-heavy background applications.
Play while plugged in and keep the vents unobstructed.

LAUNCHER OPTIONS
----------------
720p launcher:
-w1280 -h720 -window -novsync -showfps

540p launcher:
-w960 -h540 -window -novsync -showfps

These command-line switches are documented for the original PC release.
If your edition ignores them, use the in-game graphics menu.

NO UNSAFE TWEAKS
----------------
This pack does not edit the registry, disable security, replace DLLs,
modify game files, or overwrite the game's own settings/save files.
